How the Model & Solver Work

Toggle between plain-English economic intuition and the underlying mathematical equations.

01 // WHY HANK INSTEAD OF RANK OR TANK?

A Living Wealth Distribution vs. Frozen Buckets

RANK pretends the economy is one rich clone; TANK splits people into two permanent buckets ("savers" vs. "spenders") that never cross. In HANK, households face ongoing wage ups and downs and a credit limit ($\underline{a}$), creating precautionary savings and realistic Marginal Propensities to Consume (MPCs).

$$V_t(a, y) = \max_{c, a' \ge \underline{a}} \left\{ \frac{c^{1-\gamma}}{1-\gamma} + \beta \sum_{y'} \Pi(y, y') V_{t+1}(a', y') \right\}$$
02 // WHY DOES DOLLAR INVOICING ($\phi$) MATTER?

Border Price Stickiness & Expenditure Switching

When trade is invoiced in US dollars ($\phi = 1$), a domestic currency depreciation does not make exports cheaper to foreign buyers and does not immediately raise border import prices—shielding domestic CPI inflation from US Fed hikes while raising the output cost of domestic rate hikes.

$$\pi_t^M = -(1-\phi)\Delta \hat{e}_t, \qquad \hat{Y}_t = \hat{C}_t - \alpha_M\eta(2-\phi)\hat{e}_t$$
03 // FAKE-NEWS JACOBIANS (SSJEngine.jl)

Mapping Future Rate News into Today's Spending

Instead of re-simulating thousands of households for every possible shock, the Fake-News algorithm measures how a one-time news announcement at horizon $s$ ripples through the wealth distribution, assembling exact $60 \times 60$ response matrices $\mathcal{J}_{C,r}$ and $\mathcal{J}_{C,Y}$.

$$\mathcal{J}_{t,s} = \mathcal{J}_{t-1,s-1} + (\partial c_0 / \partial y)' (\Lambda^*)^{t-1} \mathcal{F}_{1,s}$$
04 // EXACT GENERAL EQUILIBRIUM SOLVER

Clearing Supply and Demand to 16 Decimal Places

Because consumption depends on income and interest rates while income depends on consumption and net exports, we stack all 60 quarters into a single linear system and invert it directly—clearing goods markets to machine precision ($\sim 1.1 \times 10^{-16}$).

$$\begin{bmatrix} I - \mathcal{J}_{C,Y} & -(\mathcal{J}_{C,r} - \chi_e \Phi_{e,r}) \\ \Phi_{r,Y} + (1-\alpha_M)\Phi_{r,\pi}\Phi_{\pi^H,Y} & I + \alpha_M \Phi_{r,\pi}\Phi_{\pi^M,r} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} d\hat{Y} \\ dr \end{bmatrix} = -H_Z dZ$$

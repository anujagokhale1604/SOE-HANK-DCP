1. Domestic Tightening: Expenditure-Switching & Import Pass-Through

When the domestic central bank tightens by 100bp, the currency appreciates ($+1.044\%$ under DCP vs. $+0.415\%$ under PCP). Under PCP, complete exchange-rate pass-through ($\pi_t^M = -\Delta \hat{e}_t$) lowers import prices and cushions the output drop ($-0.057\%$). Under DCP, dollar-invoiced import prices remain rigid, concentrating contractionary real-rate pressure on domestic output ($-0.373\%$).

2. US Fed Spillover: Inflation Insulation vs. Muted Export Boom

A persistent 100bp US Fed hike triggers a sharp domestic depreciation ($-4.168\%$ under DCP vs. $-5.552\%$ under PCP). Under PCP, complete border pass-through sparks a $+1.732\text{ pp}$ CPI inflation surge and a $+5.938\%$ expenditure-switching boom. Under DCP, sticky USD border prices cut imported CPI pass-through by roughly 75% ($+0.412\text{ pp}$) while halving the expenditure-switching elasticity $\chi_e(\phi) = \alpha_M\eta(2-\phi)$.

Model & Computational Architecture

Built from first principles in Julia to combine heterogeneous-agent distributional dynamics with exact linear sequence-space general equilibrium.

01 // HOUSEHOLD BLOCK (FastEGM.jl)

Zero-Allocation EGM & Ergodic Distribution

Solves incomplete-markets consumption-savings over a 150-point asset grid and 5-state Rouwenhorst income chain ($a' \ge \underline{a}$) with pre-allocated workspaces and Young (2010) non-stochastic lottery transitions.

$$c_t^{-\gamma} \ge \beta (1+r_{t+1})\mathbb{E}_t[c_{t+1}^{-\gamma}], \quad a_{t+1} \ge \underline{a}$$
02 // SEQUENCE-SPACE JACOBIANS (SSJEngine.jl)

Full Non-Toeplitz Fake-News Algorithm

Constructs full $60 \times 60$ household Jacobians $\mathcal{J}_{C,r}$ and $\mathcal{J}_{C,Y}$ via backward policy induction and forward distributional iteration $\mathcal{F}_{t,s} = (\partial \Lambda_0 / \partial X_s)' D^*$, capturing both Euler anticipation and wealth redistribution.

$$\mathcal{J}_{t,s} = \mathcal{J}_{t-1,s-1} + (\partial c_0 / \partial y)' (\Lambda^*)^{t-1} \mathcal{F}_{1,s}$$
03 // OPEN-ECONOMY DAG (open_economy_dag.jl)

Exact Matrix Inversion & DCP/PCP Pricing

Couples the inertial Taylor rule, damped UIP, Gopinath et al. (2020) import pass-through, and NKPC into a $2T \times 2T$ linear system solved via direct inversion to machine precision ($\sim 10^{-16}$).

$$\pi_t^M = -(1-\phi)\Delta \hat{e}_t, \quad \hat{Y}_t = \hat{C}_t - \alpha_M\eta(2-\phi)\hat{e}_t$$

Figure 1: Stationary Wealth-Income Distribution $D^*(a, y)$. Ergodic distribution computed via Young's (2010) non-stochastic lottery method, showing mass accumulation near the borrowing constraint among low-productivity households.

Figure 2: Full Sequence-Space Consumption Jacobian $\mathcal{J}_{C,r}$. Non-Toeplitz $T \times T$ derivative matrix mapping anticipated real interest rate shocks at horizon $s$ into aggregate consumption responses at date $t$.
